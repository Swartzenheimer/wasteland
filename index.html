NPCs THAT TALK

Roaming gangs

Bandit and scientist gangs roam the map. They fight each other, hunt players, and talk trash as they close in. Hostile NPCs drop scrap.

Bandit strongholds

Raidable NPC bases spawn around the map, even outside the Purge. Bring explosives for the locked doors. Tougher bases, better loot.

Settler camps

Camps along the roads, marked on your map. Each settler gives you a free gift once a day. Shoot one and the whole camp comes after you.

The Juggernaut

Something big walks the wasteland. It carries up to 100 scrap if you can put it down.

RULES

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech or spam.
  • Don't build to block monuments, roads or other players' bases.
  • Don't exploit bugs. Report them to an admin.
  • Outside the Purge, players and bases are protected. During the Purge, anything goes.
  • Bases decay like vanilla. Keep your TC stocked.
  • Map and blueprints wipe on the first Thursday of every month.
